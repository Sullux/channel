  - say you were choosing a color to paint a room:
    - all of your life experiences are in the weights
    - all of your preferences are in in the weights
    - your mental model of the room is in the weights
    - even the question "what color?" is weights
    - when we apply the question weights to your model weights, we come up with the weights corresponding to a color: that is how you choose
  - if we downloaded all of the weights in your brain onto a disk as numbers, that disk is your model
